### Tales

|Tale|Hints|
|---|---|
|Beacon of Hope|• Rush a Logistics Bay to scout and connect the Beacon (Trailway/Skyway), then repair it (~200 Heatstamps / 100 Prefabs).<br>• Always accept the hospital challenge — keep refugee deaths low.<br>• Before the finale, bank 35–50 wks of heat/food and ~160 free housing (pre-build housing districts but leave them powered off until the final wave).|
|Apocalyptic Whiteout|• A fuel/heat marathon — over-stockpile fuel and max insulation research before the storm.<br>• Random heat leaks appear during the 120-week whiteout.<br>• Keep Guard/Frostland teams free to plug them and switch off non-essential districts.<br>• Aim to lose well under half your population.|
|Depleted Cores|• You start with no Steam Core — beeline the Frostland: Logistics District on an Old Waystation, launch expeditions, rebuild the IEC Core Factory for unlimited cores.<br>• A whiteout hits ~80 weeks after you dismantle the first IEC convoy — stockpile heat/fuel ahead of it.<br>• Geothermal starts and Venturers ease the exploration.|
|Doomsayers|• The hostile faction arrives after the first whiteout — keep Tension low all game and invest early in security (Watchtowers, Guard Squads, Prisons).<br>• Curb the enemy faction's Fervour.<br>• Legionnaires' order perks help.<br>• Avoid letting a second crisis overlap it.|
|Plague|• Build hospitals early and rush vaccine research.<br>• Quarantine contaminated districts immediately.<br>• Keep Disease/Squalor from hitting Overwhelming.<br>• Menders (healthcare) or Technocrats (faster research) are strongest.<br>• Roomy maps ease quarantine.|

### Maps

|Map|Hints|
|---|---|
|Windswept Peaks|• Forgiving default map with no gimmick — ideal for learning.<br>• Rugged terrain pushes you to zone along the edges, especially late-game.|
|Crater|• New London reskin — you already know the layout, and coal deposits are larger than in Story Mode.<br>• Plan ahead for the inevitable coal dip.<br>• You can fully research the Idea Tree here (almost a New Game+).|
|Sleeping Mountain|• Sheltered valley, well-protected from the weather.<br>• All resources except oil, and coal-rich.<br>• Large but roomy — deposits sit close together, so you can still build compactly.|
|The Pit|• Fixed (non-random) layout: the pit itself causes heavy Disease and the mountain costs ~double materials — don't build in either early.<br>• Has oil, steam, coal, food and materials from the start.<br>• Heat Stamps are the bottleneck — pass Heat Auctions / Heatpipe Watch early.<br>• Research two levels of Housing Insulation (plus working-district insulation) and Triple-Ring piping.<br>• First whiteout ~week 350, so wait it out before founding colonies.|
|Forsaken Valley|• Vast frozen forests give strong Materials and there's surface oil for heat.<br>• Balanced mid-size start with room to isolate trouble spots.|
|Fractured Gorge|• Build on a semicircle — the gorge blocks the north.<br>• No fuel deposits: ride the geothermal, so only take this map if you're comfortable with how geothermal works.|
|Twilight Waters|• Volcanic-themed map — brace for tremor and lava shock events (amplified by the Volcanic Night mod).<br>• Keep districts compact and hold reserves.<br>• Over-extended sprawl gets punished.|
|Jagged Bay|• Exposed to open frostland winds (extra cold pressure) but resource-rich.<br>• Small footprint — tight resource discipline, and plan roomy quarantine space.|
|Hanging Rock|• Winterhome ruins — balanced resources but tight geography.<br>• Loses momentum as deposits dry up.<br>• Tech-rush efficient buildings to stay ahead.|
|Dreadnought|• Plenty of oil but no food — get Frostland Teams foraging immediately and favour Adaptation.<br>• A canyon is great for Housing Districts.<br>• Sustain other resources by frostbreaking.|
|Broken Shore|• Resource-rich (rare oil) but deposits sit far from the generator and there's no meaningful iron.<br>• Lean on district synergy and use warmth economically until you find iron.|
|Horizon|• Huge XL map with excellent but distant resources.<br>• The open plain negates district heat-grouping (wind chill).<br>• Spread out to pull resources in faster, stack districts where you can, and court every faction bonus.|

### Factions

|Faction|Hints|
|---|---|
|Overseers|• _Progress · Merit · Tradition_<br>• **Perk:** crime reduced citywide.<br>• **Action:** Overdrive Output — a temporary production boost across _every_ resource (food, fuel, goods, prefabs).<br>• Shines when scaling a big city (Metropolis) or racing a stockpile (Prosperous Future).<br>• The strongest all-round pick — the crime perk also spares you early Watchtowers/Prisons.|
|Bohemians|• _Adaptation · Equality · Reason_<br>• **Perk:** periodically boosts relations with your other communities.<br>• **Action:** Hold mindshaping sessions — raises Trust.<br>• A Trust and tension safety valve for crowded or politically tense runs (Beacon influx, Doomsayers pressure).<br>• Weakest on raw output — take it when low Trust is the bottleneck, not when you need resources.|
|Venturers|• _Progress · Merit · Reason_<br>• **Perk:** increased Heatstamp income.<br>• **Action:** Finance mercenaries — recruits extra Guard Squads.<br>• Fuels everything Heatstamps buy — research, Frostland ideas, expeditions — so it's ideal for Depleted Cores rebuilds and Repopulate colony pushes.<br>• Heatstamp income compounds all game.<br>• The mercenary action doubles as cheap late security.|
|Menders|• _Adaptation · Equality · Tradition_<br>• **Perk:** faster expeditions.<br>• **Action:** Organize rescue operations — a large one-time population boost.<br>• Go-to for health-heavy runs (Plague) and refugee waves (Beacon).<br>• The population injection recoups whiteout or civil-war losses.<br>• Faster expeditions also suit Repopulate — quicker colony scouting between whiteouts.|
|Technocrats|• _Progress · Equality · Reason_<br>• **Perk:** reduced Materials demand.<br>• **Action:** Optimize Research — a big research-speed boost.<br>• Best on scarce maps and research races (Plague vaccine, insulation before a whiteout).<br>• The Material savings free capacity for goods/prefabs.<br>• Situational — the research action only pays off if Heatstamp income can feed back-to-back ideas.|
|Icebloods|• _Adaptation · Merit · Tradition_<br>• **Perk:** increased Food production.<br>• **Action:** Lead volunteer explorations — a Food boost from foraging the frostland.<br>• The freeze and food-poor-map specialist (Apocalyptic Whiteout, Dreadnought).<br>• The action is a lifesaver when Food Districts stall.<br>• Curry favour early on any hunger-prone run — the passive alone eases whiteout food crunches.|
|Proteans|• _Adaptation · Merit · Reason_<br>• **Perk:** Disease reduced citywide.<br>• **Action:** Coordinate Patient Care — sick citizens keep working instead of idling.<br>• A softer Disease answer than Menders on Plague/Beacon.<br>• Especially strong when whiteout heat scarcity spikes illness.<br>• Longer-lived than most factions, since disease keeps coming from storms, housing, squalor and laws.|
|Legionnaires|• _Progress · Equality · Tradition_<br>• **Perk:** extra Guard Squads citywide.<br>• **Action:** Raise Prefabs — generates prefabs.<br>• The anti-Doomsayers pick — extra Guards hold order through hijacks and protests, while prefabs unblock early expansion.<br>• Prefab value fades once you have enough Industrial Districts.<br>• Strongest early or during unrest.|

### Ambitions

|Ambition|Hints|
|---|---|
|Repopulate the Frostland|• Rush a Logistics District on an Old Waystation, then keep expeditions running to found colonies.<br>• Research Frostland ideas (Vanguard Logistics Bay, Survivalists' HQ) for range and team output — every colony widens your resource base.|
|Develop a Metropolis|• Never let a problem (Hunger/Cold/Disease/Squalor/Crime) hit Overwhelming — growth drops to 0%.<br>• Pass growth laws (Relationship Rotation, Mandatory Procreation, Birthing Programme) and scale Watchtowers/Prisons as crime rises with population.|
|Build a Prosperous Future|• Target ~100k each of Fuel/Food/Materials/Goods — put Stockpile Hubs beside their districts for the proximity workforce bonus.<br>• Overbuild past demand and keep expeditions feeding the surplus.<br>• Demand climbs as population grows.|
|No Ambition|• Sandbox — no win condition, so set your own goals or just survive.<br>• Good for learning a new Tale or map without ambition pressure.|
