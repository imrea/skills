A curated, community-tested set of playable Utopia Builder setups (PS5).

Every row is **guidance, not a recipe** — the Suggested Ambition and any Mods are optional toggles you can drop at the setup screen for a lighter run. What each row really fixes is the pairing of **Map, Tale, Communities and Faction**.

Difficulty is deliberately absent: any preset works on any row. **Serenity Mode** (a softener) and **Survivor Mode** (no-reload permadeath) are likewise global toggles — stack either onto any setup here. Only **Volcanic Night** appears in the table, because it adds mechanics rather than scaling them.

Rows are ordered by map, roughly easiest to hardest, and **the first row for each map is its approachable entry** — so if you want to try a new map, take its first listing. ★ marks a row that reproduces the game's own recommended setup for that Tale: its suggested Communities, Map and Ambition.

**Choosing a Faction.** The three Communities you pick decide which Factions you may then force at setup:

|Community|Unlocks|
|---|---|
|Machinists|Overseers · Bohemians|
|Foragers *or* Merchants|Venturers · Menders|
|Labourers *or* Thinkers|Technocrats · Icebloods|
|Lords|Proteans · Legionnaires|

Every row below respects that gate. See `Wiki.md` for what each Faction does and how the ideology axes drive relations.

### Where to start

- **New map** — its first listed row.
- **New Tale** — its ★ row: `#1` Beacon of Hope · `#8` Apocalyptic Whiteout · `#11` Doomsayers · `#13` Depleted Cores · `#16` Plague.
- **New Ambition** — `#2` Repopulate the Frostland · `#1` Develop a Metropolis · `#3` Build a Prosperous Future.
- **Want it easier** — drop the Suggested Ambition, or turn on Serenity.

## 2-Tale Scenarios

The hard tier: two crises overlap, so every row is a genuine stack. Eight curated pairs rather than all ten — `Depleted Cores · Plague` puts two research races in competition for the same lab throughput on a city with no heat security, and `Depleted Cores · Doomsayers` lands the hijack in the window where you can least absorb it. Neither is realistically completable.

## Playthrough Hints

### 1-Tale Scenarios

|#|Scenario|Hints|
|---|---|---|
|1|Beacon of Hope · Windswept Peaks|• The game's own recommended opener, and the gimmick-free map makes it the cleanest place to learn refugee surge-planning.<br>• Icebloods' Food sustains each incoming wave and their cold adaptation cushions the whiteout finale.|
|2|Depleted Cores · Windswept Peaks|• Two Old Waystations on the most forgiving map make this the gentlest colonisation start.<br>• Rebuild the cores first, then reuse the same expedition muscle for the colony push — Venturers accelerate both.|
|3|Beacon of Hope · Crater|• Known New London ground, but the coal dip lands while you're also banking housing and heat for the wave — pre-empt it.<br>• Technocrats' lower Material demand makes the overbuild toward 100k far cheaper.|
|4|Doomsayers · Crater|• Crater's roomy known layout gives space to isolate the hijack while the population climbs.<br>• Bohemians defuse it politically — mindshaping keeps relations warm where Legionnaires would answer with guards.|
|5|Apocalyptic Whiteout · Crater|• Crater's oversized coal is exactly the fuel bank a 120-week freeze wants — spend the head start on stockpiling, not expansion.<br>• Menders' faster expeditions and rescue operations push colonies outward once the deep cold sets in.|
|6|Doomsayers · Sleeping Mountain|• Use the sheltered, roomy map to isolate the hijack.<br>• Overseers' discipline clamps crime as the metropolis scales.|
|7|Apocalyptic Whiteout · Sleeping Mountain|• The roomy map invites spreading out — then Volcanic Night's tremors punish exactly that, mid-freeze.<br>• Icebloods' cold adaptation and Food carry the whiteout and feed the early colonies.<br>• Bank rebuild materials; lava will take districts.|
|8|Apocalyptic Whiteout · The Pit|• The recommended setup for this Tale.<br>• On The Pit's fixed layout, Overseers marshal big labour surges to seal the whiteout's heat leaks.<br>• Stockpile from the map's built-in resources toward 100k while riding out the storm.|
|9|Plague · The Pit|• The Pit's disease-heavy pit is the worst possible place for an outbreak — stay out of it and reserve quarantine space early.<br>• Proteans' citywide Disease reduction is a constant buffer while you push colonies outward.|
|10|Depleted Cores · The Pit|• The Pit's built-in oil, steam, coal, food and materials feed the stockpile — but Heat Stamps are the bottleneck (Heat Auctions / Heatpipe Watch).<br>• Technocrats' Material savings speed both the core rebuild and the overbuild.|
|11|Doomsayers · Forsaken Valley|• The recommended setup for this Tale.<br>• Isolate the hostile faction on the roomy valley and curb its Fervour early.<br>• Legionnaires' extra Guard Squads hold order cheaply, freeing workforce for the overbuild.|
|12|Plague · Forsaken Valley|• Contain the outbreak on the roomy map first, then pivot to growth once stable.<br>• Menders' healthcare keeps deaths low enough to push a large population.|
|13|Depleted Cores · Fractured Gorge|• The recommended setup for this Tale.<br>• No fuel deposits — lean on the gorge's geothermal while you rebuild cores on the semicircle.<br>• Venturers' expedition and Heatstamp boost drives both the IEC rebuild and the colony push.|
|14|Beacon of Hope · Twilight Waters|• A first look at the volcanic map without the extra mod layer — keep Beacon housing compact and hold reserves for each wave.<br>• Bohemians ease the Tension refugee influxes create.|
|15|Doomsayers · Twilight Waters|• Eruptions and a hijack at once: tremors wreck packed districts just as Fervour peaks.<br>• Legionnaires' extra Guards hold order while you rebuild what the lava takes.|
|16|Plague · Jagged Bay|• The recommended setup for this Tale.<br>• Jagged Bay is small and wind-exposed — reserve roomy quarantine space before the outbreak spreads.<br>• Menders' healthcare keeps deaths low so you can grow once contained.|
|17|Depleted Cores · Hanging Rock|• Hanging Rock's deposits dry up fast — tech-rush efficient buildings, lean on its geothermal, and rebuild cores before the ~80-week whiteout.<br>• Technocrats' Material savings stretch the thinning resources.|
|18|Plague · Hanging Rock|• Quarantine costs bite just as the deposits thin — contain first, then grow into the space you freed.<br>• Proteans' Disease reduction is what makes population growth survivable here.|
|19|Apocalyptic Whiteout · Dreadnought|• Icebloods are the answer to Dreadnought's no-food freeze — their Food and cold adaptation are your lifeline.<br>• Repopulate doubles as survival here: push Logistics and expeditions outward for food and heat security.|
|20|Depleted Cores · Broken Shore|• Broken Shore's Old Waystations and oil are ideal launch points for the core-rebuild expeditions and the colony push — the friendliest way into a difficult map.<br>• Overseers marshal labour through the iron-poor cold.|
|21|Beacon of Hope · Broken Shore|• Widely rated the hardest ambition-and-map pairing in the game: refugee waves and a growing population on a cold, iron-poor coast far from the generator.<br>• Lean on district synergy, spend warmth economically, and let Proteans lower Disease among the influx.|
|22|Doomsayers · Horizon|• Horizon's vast building room suits a large population, but its distances strain order right as the hijack lands.<br>• Legionnaires' extra Guards keep it in check.<br>• Spread out, but court every faction bonus.|
|23|Plague · Horizon|• Quarantining across Horizon's sprawl is the challenge — Technocrats' research speed gets the vaccine out before it spreads.<br>• Stack districts against wind chill while banking toward 100k.|
|24|Depleted Cores · Horizon|• Excellent but distant resources make the 100k stockpile a pure logistics problem — and Volcanic Night shakes the ground while you rebuild the cores.<br>• Bohemians' relations boost holds a sprawling, drifting city together.|

### 2-Tale Scenarios

|#|Scenario|Hints|
|---|---|---|
|1|Beacon of Hope · Depleted Cores · Crater|• Rebuild cores first for heat security, then reuse the same Logistics and expedition muscle for the Beacon — Venturers accelerate both.<br>• Crater's known layout and big coal ease the double load.|
|2|Doomsayers · Beacon of Hope · Forsaken Valley|• Refugees spike Tension just as the hostile faction stirs — front-load housing and security, keep a Tension buffer.<br>• Legionnaires' extra Guards hold order through both crises.<br>• Isolate trouble on the roomy valley.|
|3|Beacon of Hope · Plague · Jagged Bay|• Health double-whammy — quarantine each district before welcoming the next refugee wave.<br>• Menders cover both refugee health and the plague.<br>• Tight Jagged Bay means reserving quarantine space early.|
|4|Beacon of Hope · Apocalyptic Whiteout · Sleeping Mountain|• Both crises demand massive heat reserves and pre-built (powered-off) housing — over-stockpile fuel on the sheltered map.<br>• Overseers marshal the labour surges that seal heat leaks and absorb refugee waves.|
|5|Depleted Cores · Apocalyptic Whiteout · The Pit|• Rebuild cores fast, then endure the freeze with minimal core support — stockpile fuel relentlessly on The Pit's fixed layout.<br>• Icebloods' cold adaptation and Food are the cushion for a core-poor whiteout.|
|6|Apocalyptic Whiteout · Doomsayers · Windswept Peaks|• A neutral map keeps the focus on the overlap itself — economise heat via district synergy and keep Tension low so the hijack doesn't land mid-whiteout.<br>• Legionnaires' Guards hold order through it.|
|7|Plague · Doomsayers · Horizon|• Contain the plague fast — unchecked Disease feeds the Tension the hijack thrives on.<br>• Menders speed containment.<br>• Spread across Horizon but court faction bonuses to hold order.|
|8|Plague · Apocalyptic Whiteout · Broken Shore|• Expert stack — a plague running into the 120-week freeze on an iron-poor coast.<br>• Bank huge fuel and food ahead of both.<br>• Technocrats speed the vaccine and insulation tech.<br>• Keep hospitals powered right through the whiteout.|
