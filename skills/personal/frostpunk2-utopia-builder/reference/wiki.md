Since the Fractured Utopias DLC you **force** your Faction at setup rather than waiting for it to emerge — but the three Communities you picked decide which Factions are on the menu. The table above is that gate: pick Machinists and you may force Overseers or Bohemians, pick Lords and you may force Proteans or Legionnaires, and so on. Each Faction then carries a 12-node **Utopia Tree**, and the fully-opposed Faction turns up later as your rival.

### Faction ideologies

Separately from availability, every Faction holds one position on each Zeitgeist axis. This governs *relations* — actions supporting a Faction's ideology raise standing with it, and steering policy toward your own Faction's positions is how you earn Utopia Tree progress. The eight Factions are exactly the eight possible combinations, so each has one fully-opposed rival.

| Faction | Technology | Economy | Society | Rival |
| ------- | ---------- | ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Bohemians | Adaptation | Equality | Reason | Overseers |
| Icebloods | Adaptation | Merit | Tradition | Technocrats |
| Legionnaires | Progress | Equality | Tradition | Proteans |
| Menders | Adaptation | Equality | Tradition | Venturers |
| Overseers | Progress | Merit | Tradition | Bohemians |
| Proteans | Adaptation | Merit | Reason | Legionnaires |
| Technocrats | Progress | Equality | Reason | Icebloods |
| Venturers | Progress | Merit | Reason | Menders |

A Community's own ideology need not match the Faction it unlocks, and the game's recommended setups routinely pick both ends of an axis (`Lords + Thinkers`, `Machinists + Foragers`) — a balanced Zeitgeist is a normal starting position, not a mistake.

## Zeitgeist

The **Zeitgeist** ("spirit of the time") is the city's evolving belief system, tracked on three opposed ideology axes. Your three starting Communities set where the city leans; as it takes enough steps toward one ideology, a Faction embodying it forms — drawing members from the Communities that share that ideology. Every Faction holds one position from each axis.

| Axis | Positions (Community) |
| ---- | --------------------- |
| Technology | Progress (Machinists) ↔ Adaptation (Foragers) |
| Economy | Merit (Merchants) ↔ Equality (Labourers) |
| Society | Reason (Thinkers) ↔ Tradition (Lords) |

Laws, buildings, and event choices all shift the Zeitgeist, and groups resist anything that opposes their worldview — so steering these leanings is how you keep the Council on side. Push a single position to its maximum to unlock its **Cornerstone**, a powerful ideology-defining ability.
